# Player Journeys: Insights

Three findings from the **demo dataset** (300 matches on 3 maps, March 3–4, generated by
`datagen/`). The data is synthetic, so these show how the tool supports this kind of analysis;
the numbers describe the demo matches, not a real game. All figures can be reproduced in the
tool (Heatmaps → tick all matches → hover) or from `web/public/data`.

---

## Insight 1: Player combat is overwhelmingly against bots, even with other humans present

### Evidence

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Matches with 2 or more human players | 243 of 300 (81%) |
| Player kills on bots (`BotKill`) | 2,723 |
| Player kills on other players (`Kill`) | 158 (5.5% of all player kills) |
| How players' matches ended (916 human journeys) | 438 extracted (48%) · 223 killed by bots · 161 killed by players · 94 storm |

### Key finding

Most matches have several humans, yet only about 1 in 18 player kills is player-vs-player.
Bots decide most fights, and they kill more players than other players do.

### Why a level designer should care

Map layout controls how often humans meet. If player-vs-player tension is the intended core of the
game, wide maps where players can travel apart (plus plentiful bots) make it rare.

### Potential design action

Add shared objectives or high-value loot spots that pull players onto the same routes; review bot
placement so bots don't absorb most encounters.

### Metrics affected

PvP kill share, time to first player encounter, deaths by cause.

## Insight 2: On Saltmarsh Hollow, the bridges are the chokepoints, and the coast is nearly empty

### Evidence

Player positions on Saltmarsh Hollow (all 150 matches), compared with how much of the map each area covers:

| Area | Share of the map | Share of player positions | Over-use |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Bridges (roads over the river) | 0.37% | 3.4% | about 9× |
| Roads overall | 7.2% | 39.4% | about 5× |

Traffic by place (different players; 438 player journeys):

| Place | Player visits | Share of journeys |
|---|---:|---:|
| Pump Station (central crossroads by the river) | 166 | 38% |
| Salt Flats Depot | 158 | 36% |
| Old Cannery | 146 | 33% |
| Lighthouse Point (far coast) | 29 | 7% |
| Radio Mast (far edge) | 24 | 5% |

### Key finding

Players cross open ground freely, but the river funnels them: the few bridge tiles carry about
9 times their share of movement, and the crossroads next to them (Pump Station) is the most visited
place. Lighthouse Point and Radio Mast are close to dead zones.

### Why a level designer should care

Chokepoints decide where fights and ambushes happen and make routes predictable; areas almost nobody
visits are wasted space.

### Potential design action

Add a ford or a second bridge to split the flow, or add cover at the existing bridges; give
Lighthouse Point and Radio Mast a reason to go there (a strong loot room or an extraction point) or
shrink the playable edge.

### Metrics affected

Traffic spread across places, share of movement through chokepoints, encounter locations.

## Insight 3: The best loot is at the edges, where the storm catches players

### Evidence

| Place | Visits | Loot pickups | Loot per visit | Storm deaths |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Harbor Works (south-west edge) | 134 | 2,140 | 16.0 | 8 |
| Salt Flats Depot (east) | 158 | 1,863 | 11.8 | 7 |
| Pump Station (centre) | 166 | 791 | 4.8 | 0 |

Harbor Works has the most loot per visit on the map and the most storm deaths at any place (8 of the
map's 48); the central Pump Station has none.

### Key finding

The highest reward sits far from the centre, and players who loot there long are the ones the storm
catches. Risk comes from the storm, not from other players.

### Why a level designer should care

Risk and reward are linked, but through travel distance rather than combat. That can feel unfair
(dying to the storm after a long loot) rather than tense.

### Potential design action

Signal the storm's direction earlier, add an extraction point near Harbor Works, or move some
high-value loot towards contested central places.

### Metrics affected

Storm deaths per match, loot per visit by place, extraction rate.

---

### How the numbers were calculated

Places are taken from the map layouts in `datagen/maps.py`; an event counts for a place when it is
within the place's radius. Visits count each player once per place per match. Bridges and roads
are the terrain cells saved with each map (`<Map>_layout.json`). Bot-vs-human
classification and duplicate removal follow the pipeline rules (see ARCHITECTURE.md §4).
